# Clips, not embedded players

The home page's Wheel shows moving video, so Videos now appear as Clips: silent five-second loops cut from each Video's hook and hosted on the site, each linking out to TikTok or Instagram. We still never embed a TikTok or Instagram player. Embeds load roughly 1 to 2 MB of third-party script per Video, set tracking cookies (a cookie banner in the EU, which the site avoids by loading no third-party code), and are fixed boxes that cannot be tilted, turned on the Wheel, muted or paused out of view. Clips keep the page light on phones, where most visitors arrive from DMs and the bio link, and the full views still count on TikTok and Instagram.

Edouard chose this on 18 Nov 2026 after weighing real embeds.
