{# The Ed's Daily signup form, shared by the home page and the /daily/ pages. The caller sets `eds_daily_source` ("home" or "daily-archive") before including it. A plain form: without JavaScript it posts to the endpoint ([extra] eds_daily_subscribe in config.toml), which answers with a redirect to /daily/check-your-inbox/ or to /daily/?error=... static/js/eds-daily.js sends it with fetch instead and shows the answer in the status line, so the visitor stays on the page. The messages are held here, one per answer of the endpoint. `company` is a honeypot: off-screen and out of the tab order, people leave it empty. #}
