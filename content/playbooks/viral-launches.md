{%<part n={1} id="part-1" title="Plan the launch">%}
{%</part>%}

{%<step n={1}>%}Write a short company brief: product and URL, target customer, problem, competitive advantage, verified founder background and evidence you can show. ICP means your ideal customer profile; CTA means the next action, such as trying the product.{%</step>%}

{%<step n={2}>%}Choose one CTA and test its full journey on a phone. Update your X bio, product link and pinned post. Publish useful demos or observations before launch and join relevant conversations.{%</step>%}

{%<step n={3}>%}Study three relevant launches in the [Doomers directory](https://doomers.ai/launches) and read its [playbook](https://doomers.ai/playbook). Note the opening claim, demo, creator angles and follow-ups. Treat the view counts as a dated snapshot.{%</step>%}

{%<step n={4}>%}Set the date, time zone, total budget and launch owner. Keep a spreadsheet with creator, email, fee, brief link, wave, calendar link, confirmation, post URL and results.{%</step>%}

{%<part n={2} id="part-2" title="Write and record the video">%}
Use this prompt for the product launch video. The target length is 2:30-3:00.
{%</part>%}

1. Founder credibility
2. Debatable claim
3. Product demonstration
4. One clear CTA

{%<step n={1}>%}Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) or [Claude](https://claude.ai/). Add your company brief and evidence. Replace every terracotta field with your information, then paste the prompt.{%</step>%}

{%<prompt>%}
Write a 2:30-3:00 launch video script for [PRODUCT], aimed at [ICP].
Company context: [COMPANY CONTEXT].

1. Introduce [FOUNDER] using [VERIFIED CREDENTIAL] to explain why they built this product.
2. State [DEBATABLE CLAIM] and explain why it matters to the audience.
3. Demonstrate [COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE] with [VERIFIED EVIDENCE] and one clear [USE CASE].
4. End with [CTA].
5. Return a timecoded script with voiceover and a visual cue for each passage. Keep the spoken read within 2:30-3:00.

Use only supplied facts. Mark missing information [MISSING] and continue without follow-up questions.
{%</prompt>%}

{%<part n={3} id="part-3" title="Find creators and send their briefs">%}
Keep the central claim consistent while giving every creator an angle that fits their audience.
{%</part>%}

{{<step_image src="playbooks/viral-launches/creator-platforms.jpg" alt="Find Paid X Creators: the logos of Passionfroot, Favikon, ViralGTM and Collabstr." />}}

Find X creators through [Passionfroot](https://www.passionfroot.me/), [Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/), [ViralGTM](https://viralgtm.ai/), [Collabstr](https://collabstr.com/). Confirm each platform’s current X offering before paying.

Review recent posts and replies for audience fit. Agree on price, format, timing and disclosure.

Paste the prompt below into your AI chat with your company brief, creator profiles, post examples and emails. Replace all bracketed fields.

{%<prompt>%}
Use [CREATOR PROFILES + EMAILS + SAMPLE POSTS] to create a unique brief for each creator promoting [PRODUCT] to [ICP].
Context: [COMPANY CONTEXT]. Shared claim: [CENTRAL CLAIM]. Action: [CTA]. Window: [POSTING WINDOW]. Assets: [ASSET LINKS].

1. Analyze each creator's audience and voice.
2. Keep the shared claim consistent. Give each creator distinct hooks, framing and format that fit their audience.
3. Include three hooks, one recommended angle, the format, CTA, posting window and paid-partnership disclosure.
4. Create one editable PowerPoint (.pptx) or Google Slides deck per creator.
5. Email each creator their own deck or Slides link with the asset links. If email tools are unavailable, return ready-to-send drafts.
6. Return a brief list: creator, email, brief link and actual send status.

Mark missing details [MISSING]. Do not invent claims or ask follow-up questions.
{%</prompt>%}

Use the brief list from the previous step. Send calendar emails separately from the briefs.
{%</part>%}

{%<row key="Wave 1 · T+15 minutes">%}Half the creators{%</row>%}

{%<row key="Wave 2 · T+4 hours">%}The other half{%</row>%}

Paste the prompt into your agent and supply the launch link, creator brief list and supporter emails. Use a named time zone such as Europe/Paris. For a 10:00 launch, the waves post at 10:15 and 14:00 in that same zone.

{%<prompt>%}
Use [BRIEF LIST], with each creator's email.
Launch: [DATE], [LAUNCH TIME], [TIME ZONE]. Link: [LAUNCH LINK].

1. Assign half the creators to Wave 1 and half to Wave 2. If the total is odd, put the extra creator in Wave 1.
2. Set Wave 1 for 15 minutes after launch and Wave 2 for 4 hours after launch.
3. Create a Google Calendar link for each creator's assigned posting time.
4. Email each creator their calendar link. Email [INVESTORS + FRIENDS & FAMILY] the launch link and both wave times so they can join the conversation.
5. Return a tracker: creator, wave, posting time, calendar link and actual email status.

Use the existing briefs. Do not create or resend them. If email tools are unavailable, return drafts. Mark missing details [MISSING]; do not ask follow-up questions.
{%</prompt>%}

{{<subhead text="Check the schedule before sending" icon="exclamation" />}}

Open a sample calendar link and verify its date, local time, time zone and launch URL. Get each creator to confirm their slot; a link alone does not prove they added the event. Track missing confirmations and failed emails.

{%<note>%}For manual setup, [create an event in Google Calendar](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/72143?hl=en) and [invite the creator by email](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/37161?hl=en). Give each creator only their own instructions. Investors and friends can join the discussion when they have something useful to add.{%</note>%}

Credit users and get permission before sharing their work. Log recurring questions, fix broken signup paths and correct inaccurate claims. Paid creator posts should include a clear [paid-partnership disclosure](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/paid-partnerships-policy).

[Every](https://every.to/contact): partnerships contact. [The Rundown](https://www.therundown.ai/submit): tool submissions or [paid advertising](https://www.therundown.ai/advertise-with-us).

{%<part n={6} id="part-6" title="Keep engaging for months">%}
Use each round of results and conversations to decide what to publish and who to work with again.
{%</part>%}

{{<step_image src="playbooks/viral-launches/engagement-loop.jpg" alt="Keep engaging for months: a loop from Review results to Repeat winning formats, Rebook top creators, Keep interacting with the community, Keep posting controversial claims, Measure again, and back to Review results. Repeat month after month." />}}

{%<row key="01 · Every day">%}Answer active conversations. Save questions, objections and examples of what people build. Turn useful themes into new posts and product improvements.{%</row>%}

{%<row key="02 · Every week">%}Repeat formats that help people understand the product. Post specific, defensible claims that people can discuss, and respond to the replies.{%</row>%}

{%<row key="03 · Every month">%}Book the creators who bring qualified traffic and product use. Give them fresh briefs and angles. Compare those outcomes alongside views.{%</row>%}

{{<subhead text="Measure the journey after the post" icon="check" />}}

1. Post views
2. Landing-page visits
3. Signups or demos
4. People using the product

Give each creator a separate tracking link. Record their fee, post URL, views, clicks, signups and activated users. Define activation as a useful product action, such as completing the first task. Compare the same window for everyone: for example, the first 24 hours and first seven days.

Signup conversion = signups ÷ measured visits × 100.\
Cost per attributed signup = creator fee ÷ attributed signups.\
When there are no signups, record that result. Links miss some later visits and shares, so treat attribution as an estimate.

{{<subhead text="Before the next launch" icon="check" />}}

Check the claims and demo. Test the product link and signup flow. Confirm brief delivery, posting times and disclosures. Assign someone to replies and product issues. Prepare the next-day follow-up.
